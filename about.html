About Us
Two generations. Two skillsets.
One team.

We're not your typical real estate team. We're family — and we bring that same energy to every client relationship.

A father and son who decided to do things differently.

Most real estate teams are just agents who share a phone number. We're not that. We're a father and son who saw an opportunity to combine two completely different approaches to real estate into something clients can't get anywhere else.

Dale has the experience, the relationships, and the kind of calm wisdom you only get from decades of working with people. Chris brings data analytics, technology, and a negotiation background rooted in communication theory from ASU West.

We run a 50/50 partnership — equal in everything. We don't play games with our clients, we don't inflate numbers to win listings, and we don't disappear after you sign. We're in it from first meeting to closing day and beyond.

The Team
Meet Chris & Dale

Christopher J. Miller

Realtor · Co-Founder

Chris handles listing consultations, comparative market analyses, offer analysis, and negotiation strategy. With a BA in Communication and Conflict Negotiation from ASU West, he brings a data-driven, tech-forward approach to every transaction. He's also an active investor and trader, which means he understands numbers, risk, and timing at a level most residential agents don't.

When he's not working with clients, he's building tools and systems to give his team an edge — from AI-optimized marketing to custom analytics dashboards.

Dale Miller

Realtor · Co-Founder

Dale is the relationship anchor of the team. He's the person clients call when they need someone to talk them through a tough decision. His experience spans decades and his communication style puts people at ease immediately. He's old school in the best way — handshake deals, follow-through, and genuine care for the people he works with.

Together with Chris, he brings the kind of balanced perspective that only comes from a multigenerational partnership built on trust.
